Leo — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

The short version: Leo has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no server of its own. Everything you do in the app stays on your device. The only things Leo downloads are game images and content updates, and it sends nothing about you in return.

Who we are

Leo is an unofficial companion app for the Grand Theft Auto games, developed and published by NorthForge Dev. You can reach us at northforgedev.io@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. Leo has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no server of its own. We do not receive your in-app activity, and we have no way to identify you.

We do receive aggregate, anonymous statistics from Google Play, which reports installs, uninstalls, ratings, and crash counts to every developer. These do not identify you to us individually.

Data stored on your device

Leo saves your in-app activity locally so the app works the way you left it. This includes:

  • Which game you last had selected (GTA V or GTA VI)
  • Saved (bookmarked) characters, missions, vehicles, weapons, and locations
  • Your story progress and Spoiler Shield settings, per game
  • A copy of the app's game content so it works without a connection

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and it is excluded from Android's cloud backup. You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Internet access

Leo uses your internet connection for two purposes only:

  • Images. Pictures of characters, vehicles, weapons, missions, and places are loaded from the community-run GTA Wiki (gta.wiki) and cached on your device so each one is downloaded once.
  • Content updates. About once a day, Leo checks this website (northforgedev.github.io) for a newer version of its game content and downloads it if there is one.

Like any request made over the internet, these downloads necessarily expose your device's IP address to the server that answers them. Leo sends no personal information, identifiers, or usage data with these requests, and we never see the image requests at all.

Permissions

Leo requests only one Android permission:

  • Internet — for the images and content updates described above

Leo does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, files, or any other sensitive capability.

Third-party links

Every entry in Leo links to its source page on gta.wiki, whose text is available under the Creative Commons BY-SA licence. These links open in your device's browser, and the sites they lead to are governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

Children's privacy

Leo is a companion app for games rated for mature audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If Leo's data practices change — for example, if a future version shows advertisements — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the effective date above will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact
