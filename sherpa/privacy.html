Sherpa — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 16, 2026

The short version: Sherpa does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Who we are

Sherpa is an unofficial companion app for the game Escape from Tarkov, developed and published by NorthForge Dev. You can reach us at northforgedev.io@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. Sherpa has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting services, no advertising SDKs, and no server of its own. We have no way to identify you and no interest in doing so.

Data stored on your device

Sherpa saves your in-app activity locally on your device so the app works the way you left it. This includes:

  • Favorited items, quests, and hideout modules
  • Quest and hideout progress, your PMC level, and stash item counts
  • Recent searches and app settings (such as theme)
  • A local copy of public game data (items, quests, hideout modules) so the app loads quickly and works with a poor connection

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and it is excluded from Android's cloud backup. You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Internet access

Sherpa uses your internet connection for exactly one purpose: downloading public game data — item details, quest information, hideout requirements, flea market prices, and item images — from the community-run tarkov.dev API.

Like any request made over the internet, these downloads necessarily expose your device's IP address to the server that answers them (tarkov.dev). Sherpa sends no personal information, identifiers, or usage data with these requests, and we never see them. You can review the tarkov.dev project at tarkov.dev.

Permissions

Sherpa requests only two Android permissions:

  • Internet — to download the public game data described above
  • Vibration — for haptic feedback in the interface

Sherpa does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, files, or any other sensitive capability.

Third-party links

Some screens link to external websites, such as community wiki pages or escapefromtarkov.com. These links open in your device's browser, and the sites they lead to are governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

Children's privacy

Sherpa is a companion app for a game rated for mature audiences and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If Sherpa's data practices ever change — for example, if a future version adds optional crash reporting — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the effective date above will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about Sherpa? Email us at northforgedev.io@gmail.com.
