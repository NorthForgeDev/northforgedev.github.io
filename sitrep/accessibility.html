Sitrep — Accessibility Statement

Effective date: August 24, 2026

The short version: Sitrep is built by one developer, not a large team, but accessibility is something we actively work on rather than treat as an afterthought. Screen-reader support, color contrast, and touch-target sizing are reviewed on an ongoing basis. If something in the app doesn't work well with the accessibility tools you use, we want to know — see Contact below.

What Sitrep currently supports

  • Labels and roles for screen readers (Android TalkBack) on navigation, buttons, list items, form fields, and toggles throughout the app
  • A minimum 44×44dp touch target on every interactive control
  • Text and interface colors checked against WCAG 2.1 AA contrast guidelines, in both the dark and light themes
  • Support for your device's dark/light theme preference, with a manual override in Settings
  • Full interface translation into 8 languages, selectable independently of your device's language

Known limitations

Raid Tool's drag-and-drop tile grid is a visual, gesture-driven layout tool and does not yet have a non-drag alternative for screen-reader users. Every item Raid Tool surfaces is also reachable through Item Search and StashTrak, which are fully screen-reader accessible — Raid Tool itself is on our list to improve.

Standard we're working toward

We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA as our reference point, adapted to what applies to a native Android app rather than a website. This is a statement of ongoing effort, not a certification of full conformance.

Contact

If you use an assistive technology and run into something that doesn't work, tell us — screen size, Android version, and what you were trying to do all help. Email northforgedev.io@gmail.com or use the feedback link in the app's Settings screen.

Changes to this statement

As Sitrep's accessibility support changes, this page will be updated to reflect it.
